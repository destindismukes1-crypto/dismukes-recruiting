How it works for workers

No fees. No runaround. No finding out the pay after you've driven six hours.

1

Call or email — that's the application

One call to (423) 523-0228. Tell Destin your trade, your certs (C7, C12, OSHA), and how far you'll travel. No portal, no account, no password to forget.

2

You see the pay before you say yes

Every job we bring you comes with the hourly rate and the per diem attached — or an honest "not stated" when the yard hasn't printed one. You decide with the real numbers in front of you.

3

You go to work — and it costs you nothing

Workers never pay Dismukes Recruiting a dime. Employers pay for placements. That's the deal on day one and it doesn't change.

Who's behind it

Built by Destin Dismukes, a blaster/painter who worked 13 years in coatings, lived on the street, and kept standing — workers apply free, always.

Destin ran blast pots and spray rigs in yards up and down the Gulf and on the road with traveling crews. He started Dismukes Recruiting out of Morristown, Tennessee, because he'd been on the wrong end of postings that hid the pay until a man was already packed. Here, the numbers come first.

Where this is headed

Every crew placed is a step toward the long goal: a wooded piece of the Alaska coast, a cabin, and a self-sufficient life lived on his own terms.

    About Destin

    Destin Alexander Dismukes is a coatings man — about thirteen years in the trade, blasting and painting his way across the country, with his own blast pot and nozzles and the tickets to prove the work: OSHA 10, C7, and C12.

    He grew up hard in Alabama, sat in special-need classes at North Mobile County Middle School where the teachers treated him badly, and learned most of what he knows by voice and by doing — not off a page. He has lived and worked in Alabama, Virginia, California, Georgia, and Louisiana, and he’s chasing inspector and QA work, Alaska in his sights.

    Now he’s building Dismukes Recruiting — a door for other workers like him. This book is the story underneath all of it.
